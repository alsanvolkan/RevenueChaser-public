The shell on a phone, in light and dark

Design mock for #184 (shell only: every board keeps its own layout inside its iframe until its own PR). What changes, and what does not:

  • Under 900 px the 240 px sidebar becomes a drawer. A ☰ button opens it; a tab, the scrim or Escape closes it.
  • The board iframe is as wide as the pane and never grown past it, so the page never scrolls sideways. A board wider than a phone scrolls inside its own frame until its PR restacks it.
  • On a phone or a touch screen every shell control is 44 px: buttons, inputs, nav items, nav headers. A mouse on a desktop keeps today's 32 / 38 / 40 px.
  • The badges and sync times move to a second, wrapping status line on a phone; on a desktop they stay in the top bar.
  • Theme: automatic from the device (prefers-color-scheme), and a toggle (Auto → Light → Dark, labelled with the current choice) stored in localStorage. Dark is today's palette, value for value.

Not in this PR: restacking the boards themselves. Each board that is wider than a phone keeps scrolling inside its own frame until its own PR; the PR for #184 lists them with their measured natural width.
